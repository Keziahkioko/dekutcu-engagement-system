Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the DeKUTCU Engagement System uses information when DeKUTCU members interact through WhatsApp.

Information we process

  • Registration details: name, registration number, gender, year of study and area.
  • WhatsApp and conversation identifiers needed to send replies and keep track of an active conversation.
  • Attendance, event RSVP, absence-reason and feedback information, with separate consent for accountability follow-up.
  • Leadership and reporting information used to manage groups, care cases and system evaluation.

How information is used

Information is used to register members, form and manage Bible Study groups, track participation, send opted-in follow-up messages, collect anonymous feedback and relayed questions, provide welfare escalation, process study-guide purchases, and evaluate the system.

Privacy and visibility

Members' own words are not shared in leader reports. Absence reasons, feedback and relayed questions are kept anonymous. Leader personal phone numbers are not shared with members. Reports are restricted to the leader roles and groups permitted in the system.

Consent and choices

Members provide separate consent for data/tracking and proactive follow-up. A member may send STOP to pause follow-up, RESUME to restart it, or use withdrawal features to remove consent and anonymise or delete applicable records.

Service providers

The system uses WhatsApp/Meta for messages, Neon Postgres for application data, Render for hosting, Groq for language classification and RAG generation, and Cloudflare Workers AI for document embeddings. Data may pass through these providers only as needed to operate the system.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained while needed for the stated engagement, reporting and evaluation purposes. Members can request withdrawal or correction through the WhatsApp bot or through a DeKUTCU leader. Anonymous evaluation records may remain after personal identifiers are removed.

Security

The system uses consent checks, role-based reporting, signed dashboard sessions, webhook verification and privacy-scoped database queries. No system can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to the project and authorised DeKUTCU leaders.

Contact

For privacy or data questions, contact DeKUTCU through the WhatsApp bot or speak directly with an authorised DeKUTCU leader.
